# Follow-up review and integrated decision release
The previous version published a series of independent charts; the new Decision Desk aligns exact country/product evidence, shows dates, benchmark screening skill, structural import and stock context, GPCP country anomaly, a selected WFP market series, and country-associated GDACS events. It never fills missing geography or commodity with another. No composite score, automated policy prescription, or causal inference. Export a dated unreviewed text brief. Provider health is separate from completeness or timeliness.
USDA reporting areas must match the selected country by exact published name; unmatched names are withheld pending a verified geographic crosswalk. Global view allows explicit USDA area selection. The WFP market selector resets with context; conflicting monthly records stay withheld. Route parameters outrank browser preferences and skip-to-content no longer modifies the hash router. GPCP map groups cells by fixed color to reduce DOM nodes while retaining the full grid.
New release gates check spatial dimensions/counts, coverage/arithmetic, evaluation confusion counts and metrics, retail observation integrity and credential states. Browser smoke includes the actual downloadable decision brief. Core deployment remains independent from refresh; additional data only appears after a validated publication.
Limitations: GPCP country means are coarse and not harvested-area weighted. Selected WFP market is not representative; latest reported month used, tied records retain stable source ordering. USDA API credentials and HDX IPC rights remain necessary; no operational alert accuracy or intervention efficacy claimed. Further work: crop area masks, real-time-vintage validation, more market and disease/logistics feeds, stakeholder evaluation.
